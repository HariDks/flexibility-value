Flexibility-value

What is a thermal battery's flexibility actually worth?

A factory making heat from electricity pays two bills: one for the power, and one to the network for delivering it. The second is usually set by the single biggest burst of power the factory ever draws, and a battery gets cheap power by drawing in bursts.

Across three electricity markets and 25 years of published prices, it was always that second bill that decided whether the battery saved money or lost it. What mattered was which hours the network watches when it looks for your biggest burst.
